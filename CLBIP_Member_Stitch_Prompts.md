## 🎨 PROMPT 0 — Design System: Gen Z Cyber-Glass Aesthetic (Gửi trước)

> **Mục đích:** Thiết lập "linh hồn" thiết kế cho toàn bộ giao diện thành viên.

```text
You are a world-class UI/UX designer on Dribbble and Apple Design Awards. I am designing the Member Portal for a Vietnamese University IT Club ("CLB IP ĐHSP Huế").
Club members are young Gen Z college students (ages 18-22) who care deeply about aesthetics, gamification, and excitement. DO NOT make it look like a boring corporate or government admin tool.

Strictly adhere to this Aesthetic Design System for all screens:
- Aesthetic Theme: Modern Neo-Glassmorphism & Vibrant Tech Hub. Sleek, energetic, and visually stunning.
- Color Palette:
  * Primary Accent: Electric Indigo (#6366F1) & Vivid Violet (#8B5CF6) gradients.
  * Secondary Highlights: Cyber Cyan (#06B6D4) & Neon Emerald (#10B981) for streaks/success.
  * Warm Accents: Sunset Coral (#F43F5E) & Electric Amber (#F59E0B) for hot badges and ranking podiums.
  * Background: Clean ultra-modern soft off-white (#F8FAFC) with floating subtle glowing colorful gradient blurs (mesh gradients), paired with frosted glass cards (#FFFFFF with subtle border: rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8), 16px to 20px rounded corners, smooth floating shadows).
- Typography: Plus Jakarta Sans or Inter. Punchy bold headings, clean metadata tags, vibrant pill badges.
- Visual Elements: Gamification elements (XP levels, flame streaks 🔥, 3D holographic badges, VIP digital student cards), smooth progress bars, rich multimedia cards.
- Language: All UI copy, labels, and student sample data MUST be in natural Vietnamese.
```
